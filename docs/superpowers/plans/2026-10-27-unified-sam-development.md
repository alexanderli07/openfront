# Unified SAM development

Goal: Let the ordinary central structure decision choose SAM preparation along with economic purchases, with a guaranteed safe coastal opening of two usable ports and one city before the first SAM.

Defense economy owns a shared development assessment: coastal baseline two income buildings and one city; inland baseline one income building and two cities. Both ports and factories count toward later income growth. Every additional two completed income building levels and two completed city levels supports another level of the main SAM. Disabled building categories do not impose impossible goals. Construction counts for initial construction order, but only completed economic building levels justify later SAM levels.

Emergency upgrades keep the interception predictor and can use the 3M reserve. Planned upgrades become structure-plan candidates, save 3M in addition to the emergency reserve, and revalidate native quotes and current observations before dispatch. Only one existing launcher is prepared; no extra launcher placement is introduced. Existing destruction recovery, disabled settings, upgrade ramp checks and observed salvo preparation remain.

Steps:
1. Regression tests for first SAM at 2 ports/1 city, level progression, mixed economies, incomplete construction, and reserve protection.
2. Shared development assessment and ordinary structure candidate/dispatch integration. Remove planned upgrades from the emergency prepass.
3. Run targeted and full tests, verify trace parentage and real quote funding, build version 1.84.73, and publish together with the tested 1,440-tile warship support change.
