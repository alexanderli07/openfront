# Posted piracy assignments

User refinement of the approved warship-hotspot design: park at a viable open-water piracy post, reassess about every30seconds, replace a destroyed posted ship at that same post when budget permits. Keep survival overrides and urgent local transport defense.

Use a monotonic wall clock for30second traffic reviews rather than game ticks. Viable posts do not switch just because another scores higher. Validate both traffic samples and patrol anchors with a bounded12tile all-water clearance disk; this rejects narrow rivers/channels and is a conservative placement heuristic, not proof of native immunity. Native paths may still traverse narrow water.

Retain vacant posts independently of the destroyed ship. Prefer an existing unassigned ship. Otherwise quote a replacement from a connected usable port, keep the economy/SAM reserve and existing ten-ship ceiling, and retain the vacancy while saving. Hold dangerous posts and avoid repeated losses with a30second replacement cooldown. Link a confirmed newly observed ship back to the old post; expire unconfirmed submissions so retries remain possible.

Routine coast/loss patrol selection cannot borrow posted ships. Only close imminent landings may interrupt a piracy assignment; nuclear dodge, low-health retreat and nearby enemy warships retain priority. Trace post geometry, reviews, vacancies and replacement decisions through the central adapters.

Validation:30second wall timing, viable-post permanence, open-water rejection, destruction/replacement lifecycle, missing money, pending confirmation/expiry, safety overrides and retained legacy behavior.
